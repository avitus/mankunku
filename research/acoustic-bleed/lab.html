How this research session works

Calibrate quietly → Hear the phrase → Count in → Play → Stay quiet for a final room measurement. Both modes use the app’s real phrase playback, samples, metronome, backing engine and scoring functions. This lab does not write practice progress.

Original microphone audio and stereo app output are captured on the same audio clock. Cleaning runs in the local Linux sandbox after the take. Saved playback remains the original microphone + app-output mix.

Keep the speakers and microphone still. A higher score does not prove better audio. Listen for missing attacks, altered notes and shifted rhythm. Calibration is experimental; no real-room guarantee has been established.
